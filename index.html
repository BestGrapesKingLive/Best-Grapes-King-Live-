🍇 How to Grow Grapes (Complete Guide)

  • 1. Location & Sunlight: Choose a sunny spot receiving 6-8 hours of direct sunlight.
  • 2. Soil Preparation: Well-draining soil mixed with organic fertilizer works best.
  • 3. Planting & Cuttings: Plant grape cuttings during February or March.
  • 4. Watering Schedule: Water twice a week initially, keeping roots moist.
  • 5. Trellis Support: Use wooden posts or wire trellises for vine support.
  • 6. Pruning Techniques: Prune old branches in winter for better fruit yield.
