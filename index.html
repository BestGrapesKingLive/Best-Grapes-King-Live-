🍇 How to Grow Grapes (Complete Guide)

  • 1. Location & Sunlight: Choose a sunny spot receiving at least 6 to 8 hours of direct sunlight daily.
  • 2. Soil Preparation: Soft, well-draining soil mixed with organic fertilizer produces the best results.
  • 3. Planting & Cuttings: Plant grape cuttings or young vines preferably during February or March.
  • 4. Watering Schedule: Water twice a week initially, ensuring water does not pool around the roots.
  • 5. Trellis Support: Use wooden posts or wire trellises to support the growing vines upward.
  • 6. Pruning Techniques: Prune old branches every winter season to encourage fresh and healthy fruit yield.
